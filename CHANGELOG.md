# Changelog

All notable changes to Transflo are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-22
The first release, on Mac, iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad.

### Mac
- Drag-and-drop wheels at the pointer: **⇧ Convert** (290 conversions across images, icons, audio,
  video, documents, PDF, slides, spreadsheets, subtitles and archives), **⌘ Tools** (31 tools in compact
  groups with Back navigation) and **⌃ Actions** (AirDrop, Archive to ZIP, Copy, Move, Copy Path, Terminal).
- Focused editors with live previews: crop, rotate, adjust, annotate, redact, add or remove a background,
  collage, compress, trim, speed, split, merge, PDF pages, metadata and rename, with audio and video playback.
- Compression with Best, Balanced or Smaller for every file type; a result is never larger than the original.
- Recipes that chain convert, compress, resize, remove metadata and rename, plus watch folders.
- On-device intelligence: text extraction (including scanned PDFs), 2× upscaling, image descriptions and
  Smart Rename with Apple Intelligence.
- Extract ZIP, RAR, TAR and GZIP into a new folder; ICO/ICNS icons; GIF compression; clipboard conversion.
- A result disc where the wheel was, a History window with Undo, a keyboard path (⌥⌘Return) and
  **Open in Transflo** in the right-click menu.
- Distribution as a notarized DMG and a Homebrew cask, with a daily update check.

### iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad
- One universal app for iOS and iPadOS 17 or later, [free on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/transflo-file-converter/id6824513929), with the
  **Convert with Transflo** share extension.
- Home with Image, PDF, Audio, Video, Text and Document; import from Photos, Files, drag and drop or Open In.
- A searchable Convert and Tools picker that shows only what your file supports.
- Native editors for images, PDF pages, video and audio, with previews and playback; Apple Pencil markup on iPad.
- Converted Files, Activity with live progress and swipe-to-delete history, and saving to Photos or Files.
- Layouts for the folded and unfolded iPhone Duo, and for iPad in portrait and landscape.

### Everywhere
- Shortcuts and Siri: Convert Files, Compress Files and Run Recipe ("Convert this to PNG with Transflo").
- English and Arabic with right-to-left layout, light and dark, accent colors, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion.
- File processing stays on your device; no accounts and no analytics.
